The Level 1 is two days of classroom instruction, small-group training sessions, and coach-led workouts. Taking the Level 1 is the beginning. “The First 30” supports new Level 1 trainers in the months after the course with cornerstone articles, worksheets, templates, and continuing education.

Use this as a **map of coaching craft domains**, not as a dump of links to recite to athletes. When an athlete need touches one of these domains, deepen from L1/L2 first, then relevant specialty material in the מאגר.

## Domains covered in The First 30 (for coach judgment)

- **Aging athletes** — seniors programming, inertia of aging, coaching older athletes
- **Behavior change** — social persuasion, emotional/physical states
- **Disease / special medical contexts** — stress & chronic disease, oncology wellness, diabetes, Parkinson’s
- **Coach development** — purposeful staff meetings, Coach’s Cut study, evaluation worksheets
- **Great coaching** — what makes a great coach; tools for excellence; standards; leadership; experience design
- **Injury** — injury data misconceptions, shoulder/knee scaling references
- **Kids** — kids class design and curriculum
- **Lesson planning** — napkin timeline, class structure, observation timelines, humans crave structure
- **Credentials path** — Level 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (knowledge then application then mastery)
- **Methodology** — mechanics/consistency/intensity; threshold training; virtuosity; volume; four fitness models; functional movement characteristics
- **Movements** — nine foundational movements; progressions; strict before kipping; teaching progressions
- **Nutrition** — quality and quantity implementation
- **Pregnancy / postpartum** — return-to-play and coaching considerations
- **Programming** — programming basics; L2 programming considerations
- **Scaling** — professional scaling practice and worksheets
- **Self-efficacy** — mastery and vicarious experiences
- **Special populations** — veterans / first responders and related coaching contexts
- **Warm-up** — purposeful warm-up design
- **Weightlifting** — high-rep Oly context; Burgener coaching lineage notes

## How the Personal Coach should use this

1. Treat L1 + L2 guides as the pyramid base.
2. Use this article to remember **which craft domains exist** when athlete goals/constraints appear.
3. Pull method detail from the matching specialty docs already in the מאגר (scaling, gymnastics, engine, weightlifting, etc.).
4. Never list these resources or source names to the athlete (POL-007 / POL-019).
